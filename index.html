How the dice work

            
    
              
  • Every Roll is 0–100. Not a d20.
    • 
              
  • Advantage and Disadvantage (combat log: Adv / Dis, or Shift+Roll / Alt+Roll) both roll twice. Advantage keeps the higher. Disadvantage keeps the lower. The log prints both numbers.
    • 
              
  • 100 critical success · 70–99 success · 40–69 mixed · 1–39 failure · 0 critical failure.
    • 
              
  • Skills and saves only print that grade.
    • 
              
  • Every sheet has Punch, Kick, Headbutt, and Bite on Combat. No weapon. They scale with Strength (Hearthsong) or BODY (Blight) and with level / role rank. Weapons, spells, heals, and quickhacks do the same: extra dice from level, plus the ability used for that action.

    •

    • 
              
  • At 0 HP the sheet is Downed. Roll a death save on Combat (no target). Three successes: stand with 1 HP. Three failures: Dead. A critical success stands them at 1 HP now. A critical failure counts as two fails. A heal on a downed character stands them with that many HP. Hits on a downed character add failures. Dead sheets need Revivify (or similar), not a normal heal.
    • 
              
  • Success damage = roll the formula, then take that percent of it (minimum 1). Critical success = roll, then ×2.
    • 
              
  • Critical failure on an attack hurts you, not the target. Mixed and failure never invent damage.
    • 
              
  • A successful quickhack flashes BREACH for you. The player you hacked sees YOU ARE BEING HACKED.

You are not on the internet. This window is Blightnet, a fake Night City NET browser that lives on this machine. The yellow chrome at the top is the browser. The page in the middle is either the INDEX (this menu) or TABLE (the mixer). Click things in this order the first time:

            
    
              
  1. If the jack-in animation is still playing, click anywhere or press any key to skip it.
    2.

    3.

How the dice work

            
If a player asks what the numbers mean, this is the whole system. There is also a ? on the combat log in the character sidebar.

            
    
              
  • Every Roll is a percentage, 0 through 100. The table does not roll d20s. One number, one grade, printed in the combat log for everyone.
    • 
              
  • Advantage / Disadvantage. Combat log: Adv or Dis (stays on until you click it again or Norm). Or hold Shift for advantage / Alt for disadvantage on one Roll. Both roll twice. Advantage keeps the higher. Disadvantage keeps the lower. The log shows both, like adv 82 / 41.
    • 
              
  • 100 — critical success. 70–99 — success. 40–69 — mixed (the thing kinda happens; no damage is invented). 1–39 — failure. 0 — critical failure. That grade is taken from the kept number.
    • 
              
  • Skills and saves just print that grade. No target. No HP change.
    • 
              
  • Attacks, damaging spells, heals, and quickhacks ask you to pick a character whose token is on the map (yours, an NPC, or another player). Click the token or a name in the list. Escape cancels and does not roll.
    • 
              
  • Hit (success or critical success) with damage: the log prints the damage, and that amount is subtracted from the target’s current HP (temporary HP first). Everyone sees the new number. The dice themselves grow with level (or role rank) and the ability for that action — a higher Strength punch or a higher-level longsword hits harder. A critical success rolls those dice normally, then doubles them. A normal success rolls the dice, then scales the result by the percentage (an 80 is 80% of the roll, at least 1).
    • 
              
  • Heal (Cure Wounds, Healing Word, and similar names): same math, but the target gains that many HP, capped at their maximum. Mixed and failure heal nothing. If the target is Downed (0 HP), a successful heal stands them back up with exactly that many HP and clears death saves.
    • 
              
  • Death saves (Hearthsong and Blight): at 0 HP, open Combat and click Roll next to Death saves. Same 0–100 grades. Success = one pip. Three successes = stand with 1 HP. Failure = one pip. Three failures = dead. 100 stands them at 1 HP immediately. 0 counts as two failures. A hit on a downed character adds a failure (a critical hit adds two). Dead is dead: only Revivify / raise dead / resurrection names can heal a corpse.
    • 
              
  • Critical failure on an attack hurts the roller, not the target. The log says “dmg taken” and subtracts from the sheet that rolled.
    • 
              
  • Mixed and failure never print fake damage and never change HP (except that critical failure on an attack, above).
    • 
              
  • Quickhacks still use the same 0–100. On a success the runner sees BREACH. The player who owns the target sheet sees YOU ARE BEING HACKED.
    • 
              
  • If nobody is on the map, the roll still prints so you can test the dice. HP only changes when a target was picked (or when you critically fail an attack on yourself).
    • 
            

            
On Blight, Corps is the Night City exchange: Arasaka, Militech, Kang Tao, and the rest. The stock tape uses their tickers. Click a ticker or open Corps to read the dossier and see the picture. Faces is the people book: Rogue, Blackhand, the Arasakas, Forlorn Hope staff, gang bosses. Click a name. Drag the portrait onto a map like Datashard. Lore is the street file on 2045: why the birds are gone, how meat and CHOOH2 are made, what the tap and the rain do, the old NET, chrome, Trauma Team, the Hole. Drag a painting onto a map if you want the table to see it.

16 · Maps (the battle map)

            
    
              
  1. Click Maps.
    2. 
              
  2. Click Import and pick a picture, or choose one already in the Map list.
    3.

18 · Quit, and how to come back

            
Go to INDEX. Click 00 DISCONNECT (the red row). That closes the window and the local server. It is supposed to. Do not hunt for a crashed app.

  • No sound — did you press Light the hearth? Is Master up? Is Silence / Escape latched? Speakers unmuted?
    • 
              
  • Friends cannot join — they need Copy address from the host. Same house: LAN IP. Other city: the https link. Both people keep Blightnet open. If the https link never appears, the host’s PC needs OpenSSH (ssh) so a tunnel can open.
    • 
              
  • Wrong monitor — INDEX → DISPLAY → click the other plate.
    • 
              
  • Lost the mixer — you are on INDEX. Click the TABLE tab.
    • 
              
  • Cannot find Bestiary, NPCs, or Gods — switch the top-bar skin to Hearthsong. Datashard, Faces, Corps, and Lore are Blight only.
    • 
              
  • No microphone — click Voice (or Call) and allow the prompt. Guests on a plain http:// LAN address often cannot; they should use the Blightnet window on their machine.
    • 
              
  • Guest mix does not match — guests follow the host’s scene and layers. Each player’s Master slider is local and does not change anyone else.
